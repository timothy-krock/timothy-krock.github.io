Lonny

I've been captivated lately by robot mowers. I've written off unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) as much less interesting than drones, because they can't travel as freely and easily. They're much less common than drones, for this reason, I think. While you might have a DJI on your shelf for taking pictures, you're less likely to have a rover. Then I saw a friend's robot mower, and it changed my viewpoint a little bit. It's a platform for autonomy tinkering. It's less stressful and constrained by energy than a drone, and it could potentially have a job! I started poking around on eBay, and immediately saw countless tracked "RC Mowers" that I thought looked very cool. I toyed with the idea for a few months and eventually pulled the trigger when I saw one at a local auction. Enter: Lonny (the robot lawnmower).

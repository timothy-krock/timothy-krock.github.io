What this edition knows.

New means first printed after November 21, 2025. Card text and Commander legality were checked with Scryfall. Avatar cards appear only as engine context. These are editorial recommendations, not a complete release review.

The independent discovery pass searched 484 inexpensive, TNT-compatible card records for tutor, untap, draw, counter and activation text. It misses cards outside that search. Selected cEDH lists and discussions supplied cross-deck leads; we have not yet ingested the full TopDeck tournament field.

Where shown, inclusion figures come from cedhstats’ October 31 snapshot of 1,248 parsed TNT lists over six months. They are source-reported, not independently recomputed. Inclusion and correlated results do not establish that a card improves the deck. “Experiment” means a testable idea, not a claim nobody has tried it.

What the counts don’t tell you

Formidable Speaker. Pitch Devoted Druid to find Hazel’s Brewmaster. Brewmaster can exile Druid and give its Food the mana and untap abilities. Discard, tutor and graveyard access all matter.

The Queen of Dale. Recruit digs for Devoted Druid or Hazel’s Brewmaster and can discard Druid when you want it in the graveyard. It does not tutor either card. That selection and discard are the reason to test it here; the Soldier is secondary.

Gleaming Splendor. A possible win outlet with repeatable white mana: make two opponents draw until they run out of library. Against the last opponent you must target yourself too, so check library sizes or protect your own draws. Infinite green alone does not pay the white cost.

Pia, Aether Ascetic. A separate name lookup found Pia in 26/1,075 lists (2.4%), below the table’s cutoff. A Dog/Thras pilot described using Pia to turn creature tutors into enchantment tutors. Applying that to Swift Reconfiguration in TNT is our inference: Pia needs a discard and puts Swift into hand; you still need W to cast it. The post is evidence for the broader idea, not for that specific target or a tournament win.
